# B1: Kinetic typography branch

15 seconds, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, silent H.264 MP4.

## Design
Large Anton headlines lead every beat. Peach Claude and blue Gemini clay cards travel alongside them. Whole assets enter with a 58% to 98% scale rise and directional travel; they leave by growing to 132% and travelling out. No asset morphing, camera movement, decorative orbit, or changing score values.

Story windows: hook 0-2.7s; Terminal-Bench 2.45-6.1s; AutomationBench 5.85-9.5s; verdict 9.25-12.35s; CTA 12.1-15s. Staggered overlapping handoffs keep multiple useful assets moving. During readable holds, type and cards continue modest opposing travel and scale progression rather than stopping.

Brand lockups remain the supplied original raster files. The background stays quiet pale warm grey. Clay depth comes from matte colour, directional shadows and light inset edges, not glass.

The source rail holds the supplied date and effort labels. From 9.5s the effort line hands off to the limited-rollout caveat. Both are readable earlier/later rather than squeezing three lines together.

## Limits
This is a typography-led exploration, not a claim of frame-exact InsiderForce replication. The shared reference study had scripts but no measured frames. It matches the hook/proof/payoff/comment-keyword structure and heavy type, while the newest requested travel direction takes priority over older no-zoom guidance. Motion is strongest at handoffs; held beats use smaller continuous motion for legibility.

Content values were preserved from the locked supplied brief, not independently researched for this branch. Brand-source caveats are retained in brand-provenance.md.
