# v9 wildcard: continuous camera journey
Spec: 15.000s, 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264 yuv420p, silent. 450 frames. Built in Python (Pillow + numpy), piped to ffmpeg libx264 crf14.
Concept: one vertical world. A virtual camera glides down through 5 stacked modules (hero > Terminal-Bench > AutomationBench > verdict > CTA). Glides 2.8-3.6s, 6.4-7.2s, 9.6-10.4s, 12.4-13.2s (smootherstep, no cuts, no zoom/spin). Each module's elements fall in as the camera lands and settle with a soft 2-step bounce. Bars grow with count-up numbers. Fake depth: background blobs at 0.30x camera speed, blurred foreground pebbles at 1.35x, extruded Anton type, clay gradients with soft contact shadows. Glass used once, on the source rail.
Scores: Terminal-Bench 4.0 Sonnet 64 / Gemini 57; AutomationBench-AA Sonnet 71 / Gemini 78 (bar length = score/100 of a fixed track, number counts up to the exact value).
Legibility: everything inside safe rect x48-888, y288-1248. Rail 50px Anton, two lines, "ARTIFICIAL ANALYSIS - 30 SEP 2026" / "SONNET MAX / ARGON HIGH" (line-split of the required string, fixed on screen). Names 60-68px, module titles 72px, verdict 160px, CTA 170-190px, lead pills 72px.
Sources/attribution: scores per parent brief (verified against the Artificial Analysis launch article by parent, not re-checked here). Anton font: Google Fonts, SIL OFL (github.com/google/fonts ofl/anton). Claude/Gemini marks are simplified drawn approximations, not the official logos. Palette/shape language from the supplied Clay tokens (clay-design-tokens). Fleet motion/3D kits attached later were not used (custom code).
Caveats: I only spot-checked frames (hero, mid-bar-growth, hold, verdict, CTA), not every frame. Pebbles at the edges are decorative and sit outside the safe rect.
