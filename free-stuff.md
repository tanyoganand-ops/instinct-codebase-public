## 1. JetBrains Student Pack
- **Claim:** https://www.jetbrains.com/shop/eform/v2/students
- **What you get:** free educational IDE licences, one year with renewal while eligible; education/non-commercial use.
- **Eligibility/verification:** genuine student enrolment at an accredited institution; use school email, GitHub Student Developer Pack or ISIC/ITIC route where offered. Real name and proof of eligibility required. School-document upload is no longer an application route as of July 2024. No card should be needed for the educational claim; do not choose a paid subscription.
- **Phone:** signup plausible; desktop IDE needs a computer.
- **Verified:** 2 Oct 2026. https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/207241195 ; https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19480141673106-Can-I-apply-for-a-free-individual-educational-license-using-official-documents-provided-by-my-school . Signup page itself is dynamic; completion not tested.

## 2. Onshape Education
- **Claim:** https://www.onshape.com/en/education/sign-up
- **What you get:** free educational cloud CAD for current students, including homeschool; academic/non-commercial use only.
- **Eligibility/verification:** genuine educational affiliation. Onshape may request proof and periodically reverify. Education is distinct from the hobbyist Free plan; check document privacy and licence terms before placing private work in either plan. No paid plan/card is needed for the education request; form completion not tested.
- **Phone:** signup plausible; mobile CAD access exists, complex modelling is easier on a computer.
- **Verified:** 2 Oct 2026 at the official signup/FAQ page above.

## 3. Unity Student
- **Claim:** https://unity.com/products/unity-student
- **What you get:** free Unity Pro Editor, Odin licence, Synty assets and Unity Cloud benefits; one-year plan renewable while eligible.
- **Eligibility/verification:** age 16+, currently enrolled at an accredited educational institution. Choose Secondary or Post-secondary correctly; SheerID verifies academic details and may request documents. Free Student plan, not a paid Pro checkout.
- **Commercial use:** allowed only under the Student terms, including the $1m Total Finances threshold for the preceding 12 months and conditions on independent student-created projects. It is not an unrestricted licence for every client/team project.
- **Phone:** application plausible; Unity Editor requires a computer.
- **Verified:** 2 Oct 2026. Product page above; full conditions: https://unity.com/legal/editor-terms-of-service/software . Account claim/card behaviour not tested.

## 4. Google Colab Free
- **Open:** https://colab.research.google.com/notebooks/intro.ipynb
- **What you get:** free hosted notebooks; GPU/TPU when available. Free runtimes can last at most 12 hours depending on availability/usage, not a guaranteed 12-hour allocation. No fixed free GPU quota or hardware guarantee.
- **Eligibility/verification:** own permitted Google account; school-managed accounts may have administrator/consent restrictions. Stay on free Colab, skip paid AI-plan/Colab upsells and pay-as-you-go. Free use does not require a paid subscription.
- **Phone:** opens in a phone browser; notebook editing/runtime management is easier on a computer.
- **Verified:** 2 Oct 2026. https://research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html

## 8. Neon Free Postgres
- **Claim:** https://console.neon.tech/signup

## Canva correction

Canva Pro/premium is **not a blanket self-claimable student perk**. Primary/secondary students need a real teacher/school invitation or school provisioning for Canva Education. University access depends on the institution's Canva for Campus offering; otherwise Canva Free remains available. No teacher account impersonation or invented school affiliation.

Verified 2 Oct 2026: https://www.canva.com/education/students/

### Ranked additions

#### 1. Deepnote Education - useful CPU notebook workspace, 16+
- Entry/instructions: https://deepnote.com/docs/edu-verification
- Benefit: free Education plan for students/educators including accredited K-12. Education FAQ grants each member unlimited Basic machine hours, up to 5GB RAM and 2vCPU. This is CPU, not free GPU.
- Verification: genuine school email and verification link, or verified GitHub Student Developer Pack. Vendor explicitly says it does not accept student IDs or other documents for this verification. Unrecognised school domains may need manual support and a couple of days. Do not assume every UK school domain is recognised.
- Age/use: terms say under-16s must not register. Education accounts are academic/non-profit, non-commercial only. Not a backend for paying clients or a commercial speech app.
- Card: Education Basic path does not need a payment method according to the FAQ distinction; Pro/Performance/high-memory/GPU upgrades require one and are excluded. Generic sign-up advertises a 14-day Team trial, so confirm Education is actually applied rather than treating the trial as the perk.
- Claim difficulty: easy if school email is recognised, medium if manually reviewed. Estimated 5-10 minutes plus email wait; computer recommended for notebook use.
- Sources: https://deepnote.com/docs/education-faqs ; https://deepnote.com/terms ; https://deepnote.com/sign-up (signup wording was search-highlight only, not flow-tested).

#### 2. Polypane - one year of responsive/accessibility browser testing
- Claim: https://polypane.app/github-students/
- Benefit: one year of Polypane free through the GitHub Student Developer Pack, explicitly for non-commercial work. Useful for portfolio responsive layouts and accessibility checks, not paid client production under this licence.
- Verification: genuine verified GitHub student account. Pack approval is a dependency, not a claim that the project GitHub account is eligible.
- Card: pricing explicitly offers a card-free trial, but that alone does not prove the student redemption flow never requests a card. Student page confirms the free year; card behaviour in the actual redemption flow remains untested. Do not use a normal paid checkout.
- Claim difficulty: easy after Pack approval, medium if Pack verification is outstanding. Estimated 5-10 minutes after verification; desktop app, not phone-only use.
- Sources: https://polypane.app/special-offers/ ; https://polypane.app/pricing/ ; https://education.github.com/pack

#### 3. Bootstrap Studio - renewable student web-design licence
- Official eligibility/entry instructions: https://bootstrapstudio.io/contact-us
- Benefit: free non-commercial student licence via school email or GitHub Student Developer Pack. Licence terms specify one year, renewable while enrolled, one computer and two hosted-site slots. Not suitable for commercial client websites.
- Verification: school email or approved Pack. School-domain acceptance and actual application prompts untested; no promise of automatic approval. The official FAQ's student portal link is the next step, not the normal buy button.
- Card/ID: official FAQ describes a free educational application, but the exact claim form was not inspected. Queue for no-card/no-ID validation at claim time, not certified as an unconditional no-upload route.
- Claim difficulty: easy-medium, estimated 5-10 minutes plus review/email. Desktop use; Windows/macOS/Linux, Chrome OS not supported according to the FAQ.
- Sources: https://bootstrapstudio.io/pages/license ; https://education.github.com/pack

#### 4. Working Copy - useful if P uses iPhone/iPad
- Claim: https://workingcopy.app/education/ (also resolves as official page at https://workingcopyapp.com/education/)
- Benefit: all Pro features free while logged into a verified student GitHub account through the Pack. iOS Git client, directly useful for reading/editing project repositories on a phone.
- Verification: install/update the app, use the education-page verification action, sign into the genuine student GitHub account. Not applicable to Android.
- Card/ID: student page describes GitHub verification rather than purchase; actual app claim/payment behaviour not tested. Do not buy the standard Pro unlock.
- Claim difficulty: easy after Pack approval, medium before. Estimated 3-5 minutes plus installation. Phone-friendly by design.
- Source: https://education.github.com/pack

#### 5. AWS Educate - free real cloud labs, UK 13+
- Entry: https://aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate/
- Benefit: free self-paced cloud learning, hands-on AWS sandbox labs and earned digital badges. This is not a cash grant, unrestricted hosting account or a pool of deployable AWS credits.
- Eligibility: UK minimum age 13. Under-18 participation requires involvement of a parent, guardian or educator under section 6.1. Main page says email-based registration and no credit card.
- Card/ID: no credit card needed is explicit. No identity-document requirement is advertised on the fetched programme page; registration was not submitted, so no universal guarantee against account-specific verification.
- Claim difficulty: easy, estimated 5-10 minutes for setup, but guardian/educator involvement adds a dependency when under 18. Labs are better on a computer. Job Board and Emerging Talent Community are 18+; do not bundle those into an under-18 claim.
- Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/legal/learner-terms-conditions/ ; https://www.aboutamazon.co.uk/amazon-for-schools/online-hub-offerings/aws-educate

#### 6. IBM SkillsBuild - school-age AI/technology learning and earned credentials
- Entry/eligibility: https://skillsbuild.org/frequently-asked-questions
- Benefit: free High School programme, designed for ages 13-18, with technology/workplace learning and IBM digital credentials earned by completing requirements. Not API/cloud spending credit, guaranteed mentorship or an internship offer.
- Verification: choose the correct high-school route and real details. The FAQ's institution-issued-email requirement is specifically attached to Software Downloads, not proof the whole learning programme requires a school email. Separate software access is not promised here.
- Card/ID: fetched FAQ describes a free account; actual registration verification, guardian consent and any ID prompts are untested. No card-backed or document-upload path is approved.
- Credential privacy: accepting/managing digital credentials requires a separate Credly account. Publishing badges or connecting LinkedIn is optional and would need P's choice. Do not advertise every lesson as a certificate.
- Claim difficulty: easy-medium, estimated 5-10 minutes for setup; earning useful credentials takes course work. Website signup may work on a phone; hands-on study is easier on a computer.
- Sources: https://skillsbuild.org/high-school ; https://skillsbuild.org/about ; https://skillsbuild.org/frequently-asked-questions

### Queue rules

Benefits tied to a school email or student GitHub account cannot honestly be claimed using an unrelated assistant/project identity. P supplies genuine personal/school details directly if he chooses to claim. Each signup still needs his separate permission. Card-free does not mean data-free: school identity, account email, GitHub profile or repository permissions can still be disclosed. Times above are practical estimates, not vendor promises. No completed claim or pending application is asserted.
