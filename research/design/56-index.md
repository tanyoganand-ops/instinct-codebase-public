## How to use this folder

Read the relevant topic before repeating research. Append a dated entry with the question, sources, findings, limits and next step. Keep owner preferences separate from research recommendations. A delivered draft is not an accepted design. A past statement that a page was live or inspected is not proof of its current state. Check current pricing, licences, feature support and links before relying on them for a new build or purchase.

This is a historical backfill, not a new product comparison or a completion update to tasks.md. Task IDs above follow the requested research buckets; their exact current task descriptions were not inspected during this backfill. No task has been marked done.
