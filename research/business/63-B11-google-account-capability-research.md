## Data and permission boundary
A successful login proves session access, not permission to inspect mail or files, connect another account, publish, share or send. Confirm the specific approved account, service, data and purpose. Sign-in/identity data, account security checks, API data-use terms and uploaded project content are distinct exposure questions. No promise of zero data collection is supported.
