## 4 October 2026: CSC go/no-go prep brief

Checked official CSC pages and Devpost documentation at about 05:49 BST. This adds execution prep to the existing deadline research, not an application or signup.

### Verdict and deadline

**Conditional GO today** if P is genuinely a high-school student aged 13-18, has any required parental consent, and can spend a focused 4-7 hours on one small school-life prototype. **NO-GO** if those eligibility conditions fail, or if P cannot demonstrate and explain the project before the earlier cutoff. P's actual age/school status and existing project/submission state have not been verified. This is a good thematic fit for an AI/automation builder, not a prediction of winning.

- Schedule and main listing: Monday 5 October **00:00 PDT = 08:00 BST**.
- Written rules: Monday 5 October **12:00 PM Pacific = 20:00 BST**. The contradiction remains unresolved. Do not count on the extra 12 hours. Finish and submit Sunday evening; 20:00 BST Sunday is a suggested internal target, not an organizer deadline.
- Online, solo or team of up to four. No UK exclusion is shown; the listing says all countries/territories except standard exceptions. Rules specifically require high-school students aged 13-18, not all students. University status alone does not qualify.
- Devpost terms require age 13+ and either age of majority, emancipation or legal parent/guardian consent. Use real details. No ID/card upload is listed in CSC submission requirements; sponsor redemption or eventual prize fulfilment can have extra conditions.

### What it is and why it fits

CSC Back-to-School is a beginner-friendly build competition for technology that improves school life. Apps, websites, AI tools, gadgets and design prototypes are allowed; an unfinished but clearly demonstrated proof of concept is acceptable. AI use is explicitly encouraged and does not lower scores, but disclose it and understand the result. Judges score learning, design, creativity, functionality and impact. Cash awards advertised: $250/$100/$50; sponsor credits are conditional non-cash benefits, not guaranteed money. No credible win probability is established.

**Recommended scope (proposal, not an existing build):** a homework triage planner. Paste fictional assignments and available study slots, extract subjects/deadlines, let the student correct errors, then generate a short prioritised plan with an explanation. One screen plus one useful end-to-end flow. Avoid a generic chatbot, automatic homework answers, student-account integrations, live personal data or a big backend. Use synthetic examples, never real school records without permission.

Substantial development must occur in the official hackathon period. Disclose any old code, project, templates, datasets or major assets; do not rebrand an old finished app as a fresh build. Credit APIs, open-source resources and AI-generated work.

### Build and submission checklist

1. **Eligibility/decision (10-20 min):** P confirms actual age and high-school status, consent if applicable, enter/skip, project choice and willingness to show a public demo. If already registered/submitted, inspect that state rather than make a duplicate.
2. **Register/start draft (10-20 min):** P signs into or creates Devpost, joins this hackathon, chooses Start project (or imports a qualifying project with honest prior-work disclosure), and adds up to three teammates if needed. Registration alone is not submission.
3. **Build one core flow (2-4 hours):** implement fictional input -> editable extracted tasks -> prioritised plan. Include a fallback/error state. Test at least three scenarios, including ambiguous deadlines. Make no unsupported claims about accuracy or proven impact.
4. **Evidence and write-up (45-75 min):** project name/tagline; who has the school-life problem and why; what works/how; learning and limits; screenshots or accessible demo; all tools/APIs/datasets; explicit AI-use disclosure; team names; source/build/design files if available. A 1-2 minute demo video is optional under CSC rules, not mandatory. A short recording showing the core flow is worth doing if time allows.
5. **Award choices (5-10 min):** select CSC Innovation Award/Honorable Mention and applicable sponsor prizes, not every box blindly. CSC awards require an open-source OR publicly viewable project plus a public link and permission for CSC to feature/promote it. Ownership stays with the creators. Render prize credits specifically require Render Workflows and an eligible redeemer aged 16+. Do not add Render just to chase credits.
6. **Submit and verify (15-30 min):** complete required fields and terms, click Submit project, check the green confirmation and Submitted status under My projects, open View and test every judge-facing link without login. Draft is not entered. Fix any validation errors well before 08:00 Monday.

**Estimated remaining effort:** ready qualifying prototype, 60-120 minutes for packaging/submission; starting from zero, roughly 4-7 hours including build, tests and buffer. These are planning estimates, not organizer timings or a guarantee. Larger integrations make a one-day entry risky.
