# Travel grants, residencies and founder houses - O03

## 27-29 September 2026: historical scope

P wanted programmes with funded travel/accommodation, including Forge-type residencies/founder houses, ideally opportunities accessible remotely. The 29 September research summary reported no suitable open paid fly-out residency among the checked Antler, Forma and Foundervine leads. This is a historical snapshot, not a claim that those organisations have no current programmes. Their live terms were not rechecked in this backfill.

## 2 October 2026: Junction correction

The earlier research called 25 October a Junction travel-grant date. Current official sources distinguish the two:

- Main Junction 2026 page: general applications close "Midnight 25.10.2026 GMT +03"; event 13-15 November in Espoo, Finland. The midnight convention was not clarified here.
- Same page: travel-grant deadline September 6.
- Official application/event page: international €300 and €100 travel-grant applications have closed.

Therefore do not promise a travel grant to someone applying by 25 October. Free event entry and discounted hotel options are not fully funded travel or free accommodation.

Sources checked 2 October: https://2026.hackjunction.com/ ; https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026

## Other scoped leads

Young Entrepreneurs Challenge advertises a Tokyo summit trip for its overall winner. That is a competitive award, not a generally claimable travel grant. See deadlines-2026-10-02.md.

Cloudflare Cold Start pays finalist flights but requires a US/Canada-based startup. A UK-only startup is ineligible under the published wording; do not treat it as a London travel opportunity. See fellowships-grants.md.

Before pursuing a residency: verify identity of programme, dates, actual coverage, deposits, visa costs, insurance, accommodation and cancellation terms. No travel application or booking was made.
