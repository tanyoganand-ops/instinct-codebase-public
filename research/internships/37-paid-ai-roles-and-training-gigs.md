## AI training gigs (hourly, remote, assessment-gated)
- Outlier (Scale AI), coding: up to $50/hr, about $200/month free GPT/Claude access, UK listed, 18+, weekly pay. Generalist track up to $35/hr. https://outlier.ai/coding , https://outlier.ai/generalists/en , terms https://outlier.ai/legal/terms-of-use . The full-stack expert track asks 3+ years experience.
- DataAnnotation: coding $50-150+/hr (home page showed $75-150+; FAQ quotes $50-75 typical), generalist $25-50/hr. UK among about 6 accepted countries per third-party reviews, confirm at signup. One-shot Starter Assessment (about 1-2 h). https://dataannotation.tech/ , https://www.dataannotation.tech/faqs
- Mercor: example STEM Uplift $40/hr open to UK citizens; software/CS roles appear regularly; degree requirements vary. https://work.mercor.com
- Alignerr (Labelbox): Code Review Specialist $40-120/hr. https://www.alignerr.com/jobs/49ad38f0-44fc-4fb6-84ea-27d738404352
- Mindrift (Toloka): coding $32-90+/hr, 18+, UK fine, payouts twice monthly. https://mindrift.ai/project/code , eligibility https://mindrift.ai/legal/eligibility-and-geographic-restrictions
