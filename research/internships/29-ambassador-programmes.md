# Ambassador and affiliate programmes

Source: ambassador research track, first-party pages fetched 2026-09-27. Cash is mostly commission, not stipend.

## Open then
1. Creatify affiliate: 25% recurring (+5% above $5,000 monthly sales). https://creatify.ai/affiliate . Campus ambassador gives Pro access, no stated stipend. https://creatify.ai/ambassador
2. ElevenLabs affiliate: 22% of paid subscriptions for 12 months (11% Business), 18+, about 90-day validation. https://elevenlabs.io/affiliate , terms https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates-terms . Ambassador is credits/access only, needs a work sample. https://elevenlabs.io/ambassador
3. GoRizzume Campus Crew: GBP 30 per video, GBP 50 per event shift; only Kingston, Durham, UCL, City St George's. https://gorizzume.co.uk/campus-crew
4. HeyGen ambassador: credits, no stipend. https://www.heygen.com/geniverse/ambassador-program , FAQ https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/14648487-heygen-ambassador-program-faq
5. Cursor ambassador: credits, no salary, 18+. https://cursor.com/ambassadors . Campus Leads closed until January. https://cursor.com/campus-leads
6. Runway Student Ambassador: current intake unverified (announcement Dec 2024). https://runwayml.com/news/runway-launches-student-ambassador-program
