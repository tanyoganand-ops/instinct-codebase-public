# Shorts monetization refresh (as of 4 Oct 2026, UK)

Bottom line: YouTube just made Shorts money harder. 270k views earns nothing on YouTube today and would be worth roughly £6-£70 even if he were monetized. TikTok Creator Rewards is the realistic first payout, but only with 10k followers, 100k views in 30 days, 1+ minute videos and age 18+.

## 1. YouTube: what changed (verified, official)

Source: YouTube Blog, 10 Aug 2026 - https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/

| | Now (until 31 Jan 2027) | From 1 Feb 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Ad revenue entry via Shorts | 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days | 1,000 subs + 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days |
| Ad revenue entry via long-form | 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours (12 months) | 1,000 subs + 8,000 qualified watch hours |
| Shorts revenue share for channels already in YPP | No stated minimum | Needs 10M qualified Shorts views in last 90 days, else Shorts earn nothing (long-form keeps earning) |
| Fan funding / shopping tier | 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views | Stated as unchanged |

- Current rules: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 (confirms 1k subs + 4k hours or 10M Shorts views/90 days; 2-step verification, no strikes, AdSense linked; review takes about 1 month; re-apply after 30 days if rejected).
- Details of the Feb 2027 changes and the activity rule: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331/ (secondary source citing YouTube help pages).
- Shorts creator keeps 45% of the allocated creator pool (YouTube blog above).
- Not verified: whether the 500-sub tier is open in the UK. The help page says it is limited to listed countries, and I did not get the list. Check "Earn" in YouTube Studio.
- Not verified: whether applying before 1 Feb 2027 locks in the old thresholds. The blog says the new entry requirements apply to new creators, and the new terms take effect 1 Feb 2027. Apply before then to be safe.
- YouTube says new incentive programs (shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives, trend boosts) will come for channels under 10M. No details yet.

## 2. YouTube Shorts RPM, UK

YouTube does not publish Shorts RPM. These are third-party estimates:
- AIR Media-Tech, data from 274 channels, Jun 2026: UK $0.166 per 1,000 views, US $0.328, Australia $0.193, Canada $0.165, Germany $0.163. https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026
- Other calculators put it at $0.01-$0.10 per 1,000 views. https://growcreator.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-2026 , https://creatorcalc.io/guides/youtube-earnings-2026.html (weaker sources).
- Working range used below: low $0.03, mid $0.166 (UK), high $0.33 (US-level, unlikely for a UK audience).
- Shorts RPM is 3-14% of long-form RPM in most niches (AIR link above). Music is the exception.

## 3. What ~270k YouTube views would have earned

Assumes all 270k were qualified Shorts views, he was already monetized, and $1 = about £0.75 (my assumption, not a sourced rate).

| Scenario | RPM | USD | GBP approx |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | $0.03 | $8 | £6 |
| UK mid | $0.166 | $45 | £34 |
| US-level | $0.328 | $89 | £66 |

- Reality today: £0. 270k is 2.7% of the 10M-view threshold, and subscribers also need to be at 1,000.
- AdSense pays out only at £60 (GBP payment threshold, table at https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14727140?hl=en-GB ; payment is issued 21st-26th of the month after the balance passes it: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7164703?hl=en-GB). At the UK mid RPM that is about 480k monetized Shorts views, so 270k would not have reached payout in the low or mid case.
- Under the Feb 2027 rules, a channel with 270k Shorts views per 90 days earns nothing from Shorts at all.

## 4. TikTok Creator Rewards: current state and UK eligibility

Official source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/creator-rewards-program
- Requirements: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, personal account in good standing, original content, videos at least 1 minute long, based in an eligible country.
- Only qualified views count: For You feed views, unique, 5+ seconds watched, no paid or promoted views. A video earns once it hits 1,000 qualified views.
- Reward = Standard (qualified views x RPM) + Additional (quality/engagement-based).
- Apply in app: Profile > Menu > TikTok Studio > Creator Rewards Program. Reply in about 3 days. If rejected, appeal within 30 days.
- UK: TikTok Creator Academy lists the UK among eligible countries, but that page is dated May 2025: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program . The 2026 official country list page did not give a usable list in my fetch. Confirm in the app (TikTok Studio shows whether the program is offered to his account).
- Payments run through PayPal/Hyperwallet per TikTok's legal terms (US page: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en ; EEA page: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en ). I could not verify the UK payout minimum from an official page. Third parties say $10; treat as unverified.
- RPM: TikTok publishes no figure and says it varies. Third-party estimates are $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (https://www.contentstats.io/blog/how-many-views-to-get-paid-on-tiktok , https://nextechads.com/tiktok-crp-thresholds-explained-what-every-creator-needs-to-know-in-2026/ ). A UK calculator site says about £0.85 (https://payoutmath.co.uk/tiktok-creator-rewards-calculator-uk/ ). Weak sources, so treat as rough.
- Illustration: 270k qualified views at $0.40-$1.00 is about $108-$270 (about £80-£200). Reality: Shorts-style clips under 60 seconds earn nothing under this program, so this only applies to 1+ minute videos.

## 5. Fastest realistic path to first payout

I do not have his current subscriber/follower counts, only the ~270k YouTube view figure. Paths below need those checked.

YouTube (slow)
1. Shorts route needs 1k subs + 10M views in 90 days. Not realistic near term, and 20M from Feb 2027.
2. Long-form route (1k subs + 4k watch hours, 8k after Feb 2027) is the more reachable YouTube route, since Shorts watch time does not count toward the 4,000 hours (help page above).
3. Even once accepted, first payout needs £60 balance, plus the review month and the payment cycle. Expect months after hitting thresholds.
4. Fan-funding tier (500 subs, 3 uploads, 3M Shorts views or 3k hours) is the earliest YouTube milestone, if available in the UK. It unlocks memberships/Super Thanks/shopping, not Shorts ad revenue.

TikTok (faster)
1. Reach 10k followers and 100k views in a rolling 30 days.
2. Post 1+ minute original videos (this is the main format change for him).
3. Apply (about 3 days). Earnings start per video at 1,000 qualified views.
4. First payout after the monthly calc and the payout minimum. Likely 1-2 months after approval, not verified.

Gating issue for both: 18+ rule for TikTok and AdSense payee. Does not matter if he already knows it is met, but worth confirming before building around it.

## 6. Ranked action list

1. Check actual numbers in YouTube Studio (subs, 90-day Shorts views, watch hours) and TikTok Studio (followers, 30-day views). Everything else depends on them. About 10 minutes, phone.
2. Confirm the 18+ requirement is met for TikTok Creator Rewards and AdSense. Zero cost, blocks both if not.
3. Open TikTok Studio and check the Creator Rewards Program tab to confirm UK availability on his account.
4. Make TikTok the first monetization target: 1+ minute original videos, reach 10k followers and 100k views in 30 days. Best odds of a first payout.
5. On YouTube, stop expecting Shorts ad revenue. Click "Get notified" in YouTube Studio > Earn, and check if the 500-sub tier is open.
6. If he wants YPP at all, apply before 1 Feb 2027 to avoid the doubled thresholds (apply once thresholds are met; confirm in Studio which thresholds show).
7. Add some long-form on YouTube. It is the only realistic route to YPP, and RPM is 7-30x Shorts (AIR link).
8. Keep a note of the YouTube "incentive programs" announcement for channels under 10M. Details were "coming soon" in Aug 2026.
