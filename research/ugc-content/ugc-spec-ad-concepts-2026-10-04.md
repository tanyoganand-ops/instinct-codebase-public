# 3 spec UGC ad concepts - GenieAI, Pallo, ThetaWave (4 Oct 2026)

Parked repo file, not for chat. Drafts only. Nothing is posted or sent to any company.

## Rules for all three
- Label each "spec sample - not sponsored" in the caption. Do not imply the company hired you.
- Film only what you really ran. Items in [brackets] are things you must fill with your real result. If you have not used the product, make the video about trying it for the first time, and say so.
- Phone, vertical 9:16, one take plus screen recording, natural light, captions on. UK register, casual, no hype words.
- Do not use competitor names, "guaranteed" claims, or fake screens.

Brief sources (read 4 Oct 2026): GenieAI https://www.genieai.co/genie-content-creators ; Pallo https://pallo.ai/ugc ; ThetaWave https://thetawave.ai/creator-program

## 1. GenieAI - "I reviewed a contract with AI" (about 30s)

What GenieAI's brief asks for (checked): legal AI for business teams. Formats include platform tutorials, honest reviews, how-tos and 60-second tips. Videos start from a brief or your own pitch, and the concept is approved BEFORE recording. Screened on craft and clarity, not followers. Publish on YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, X.
Match: this is a "tip/how-to" format, product shown doing real work, honest tone, LinkedIn-ready. Because the real product is a legal tool, this sample uses a public sample contract and says "not legal advice".
Caveat: GenieAI gives creators a free Pro account only after acceptance. For the sample, either use a free tool you really have to read a public sample contract and label it as such, or send the concept as a pitch first (their own process) and film once approved.

Script
- Hook (0-3s, to camera): "Nobody reads contracts. So I tested whether AI can read one for me."
- Demo (3-22s, screen recording over voice): "This is a public sample tenancy agreement, not a real one. I asked it: what should I worry about? [It flagged ___ clause.] In plain English that means [___]. Took about [__] seconds."
- Honest line (22-27s, to camera): "It's not a lawyer. For anything real you still check with one. But it tells you what to ask."
- CTA (27-30s): "Try it on a document you've been ignoring. Not legal advice."

Shot list: face hook at desk -> screen recording of the prompt and answer, finger pointing at the flagged clause -> face reaction -> face sign-off.
Fill-ins: tool used, clause flagged, seconds taken.

## 2. Pallo - "revising GCSE maths with a tutor that shows its working" (about 25s)

What Pallo's brief asks for (checked): natural student-style TikTok/Reels, not ads. Show how students really use Pallo; hooks under 3 seconds; show the app on screen at least once; say or overlay "Pallo"; include a CTA; show the canvas / live consult feature (write workings, get feedback). No competitor mentions, no misleading claims such as "guaranteed A". Audience 13-18.
Match: all of the above. Rate is unconfirmed (site $300/month vs a Feb recruiter post at $20 base + CPM), so get it in writing before doing real work. This sample is just a demo.
Caveat: you need real use of Pallo to film the on-screen part. If you do not have access yet, do not fake it. Film the hook and the reaction only, or wait for access.

Script
- Hook (0-3s, to camera, whisper-casual): "I got this question wrong three times. Then this happened."
- Demo (3-20s, screen recording of the canvas): "I wrote my workings out on Pallo's canvas. [It pointed at the step where I ___.] I fixed it and got [___]. It didn't just give me the answer, it showed me where I went wrong."
- CTA (20-25s, to camera): "If you're doing GCSEs, try writing your workings in Pallo, search it up."

Shot list: face close-up hook -> screen recording on canvas with the app visible -> face "oh that's what I did wrong" reaction -> sign-off with Pallo text overlay.
Fill-ins: the question, the step you got wrong, the result. Do not say "guaranteed" anything. Do not name other apps.

## 3. ThetaWave - "how I turn one lecture into a quiz" (about 30s)

What ThetaWave's brief asks for (checked): 15-60s TikTok/Reels about how you actually study with it: lecture transcription, exam prep, flashcards, podcasts, "whatever you'd actually use. Authenticity wins." They give angles and hooks, not scripts. Students, comfortable on camera, 3-5 posts a week. Apply with socials and one video idea.
Match: single real study use, authentic, short. The sample doubles as your "one video idea" in the apply form.
Caveat: base pay is unpublished and the load is 3-5 posts/week, so treat this as a sample for the form, not a commitment.

Script
- Hook (0-3s, to camera): "Revision tip nobody told me: don't re-read, get tested."
- Demo (3-24s, screen recording): "I put in my [subject] lecture. It turned it into a quiz. [I got __/__.] The ones I missed are exactly the bits I'd have skipped."
- Honest line (24-28s): "You still have to answer them yourself. It just stops you wasting an hour re-reading."
- CTA (28-30s): "Try it on your messiest lecture."

Shot list: face hook at desk with notes -> screen recording of lecture to quiz -> score reaction -> sign-off.
Fill-ins: subject, score, which questions you missed.
