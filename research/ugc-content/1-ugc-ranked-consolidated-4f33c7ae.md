## Prepare once

Use one portfolio link containing a real talking-head clip, a genuine AI-tool screen demo and a short product explanation. Label unpaid mock work "spec sample - not sponsored". Do not present AI-generated people or app screens as real paid client work. Put current channel links and analytics beside samples.

Before accepting work, agree base pay, payout date, revision limit, usage period, paid-ad rights, exclusivity and cancellation. Ask whether a test is paid. Affiliate commission, gifts, software access and competition prizes are not guaranteed UGC fees. Voice/face-clone work needs clear likeness-use and deletion terms. Finance/trading promotion needs a separate UK compliance check before publishing.
