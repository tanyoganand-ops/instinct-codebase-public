# UGC pricing research

## 2026-09-27 16:56 - first answer (agent, WhatsApp)
P asked what his videos are worth (stated ~1k followers, ~170k LinkedIn impressions, old memes Shorts channel at 300k views; unverified self-report).
- UGC (brand posts the video): followers barely matter. UK beginner £50-£150/video, median £150-£250. Suggested ~£150 for AI niche, "never under ~£100".
- Sponsored post on own account at nano tier (~1k followers): ~£20-£150/post. LinkedIn impressions help argue the top end.
- 300k Shorts views help the pitch but not the price (year old, memes).
- Usage rights: +30-50% if the brand runs it as a paid ad.
Sources: https://whito.co.uk/research/influencer-ugc-rates-uk/ and https://fueler.io/blog/how-much-do-ugc-creators-earn-in-the-uk

## 2026-10-01 - revised (task agent "UGC gig research", checked official pages 1 Oct)
- Beginner UK evidence supports £50-£150, not a universal £100 minimum. The earlier "never below £100" was too rigid.
- Proposal (not an observed market rate): quote £100 for one edited 20-45s demo with captions, one hook, one revision, limited organic use. £270 for three if batched. £75 can be fair for a tightly scoped first paid pilot. £150 is a target with good samples, not guaranteed.
- Paid-ad rights: +30-50% for 90 days (agency suggestion). Price raw footage, extra hooks, exclusivity and creator-account ad permissions separately. Avoid perpetual rights.
- JoinBrands UK: £50 default floor for UGC video, 20% free-tier fee, so £40 net at £50. Social-post video default £80 (different deliverable).
- Rates are self-reported or agency-reported, not a census, and not AI-specific.
