Estimate: 10M views is about $200-$800 (agent's estimate from sources, not a published figure; no AI-niche figure exists). Sources: https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/ , https://miraflow.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-2026-real-ranges-by-niche , https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026
