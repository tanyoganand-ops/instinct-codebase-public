# UGC programmes with a stated or possible base rate

**Source set:** UGC application master list (4 October 2026, 140 verified programmes) and its ease-of-entry resort. Tiers below are from the resort: **Apply today**, **Good odds**, or **Stretch**. This is a classification of what the supplied documents say, not a fresh verification of current programme terms or open intake.

“Base” means the programme description identifies fixed pay for a video, post, deliverable, campaign, or period, rather than relying only on views, referrals, or sales. Amounts and conditions are kept as described. A listed fee is not a guarantee of being selected or assigned work. Pure performance/affiliate-only programmes are excluded. Where the evidence does not establish a fixed base, the route is in **Ambiguous / unknown** rather than guessed into a base-pay category.
