# 30-day AI UGC content calendar + brand pitch templates

## 2026-09-27 - built by task agent "Six6 UGC plan"
Delivered as a private hosted File (https://files.instinct.com/file-01M3HEPP8ZDZAB603TJ62CQN4F) after a visual check. Programme terms were checked against first-party pages on 27 Sep 2026. This is a plan and drafts only. Nothing was posted, sent or signed up for.

Cadence: 1 post a day, about 1-2 hours after lectures. Days 7, 14, 21, 28 are light posts used to batch-film extra clips.

Rule: pitch average views per video and 90-day impressions, never the lifetime number. Cognee pays on each post's own 7-day organic impressions.

Money tags: Cognee = per-post cash; Lovable / ElevenLabs / Creatify = affiliate; Knowunity = student creator fee; Reach / Portfolio = no direct payout.

### Pitch template 1 - Cognee Creator Program

Cognee's flow is post first, submit after 7 days - so this pitch goes with a live demo link, not a cold ask. Best fit for days 1, 8, 16, 20 and 25.


**DM version** (Send on LinkedIn or X)

Hi - I'm [name], an AI builder in the UK. I build agents and automations (n8n, Make, custom code) and post demos as [handle] - [90-day impressions] impressions in the last 90 days. I just posted a demo of [one-line build description]: [link]. I'd like to submit it to the Creator Program once it hits 7 days - anything else you need from me?


**Email version** (To their creator contact)

Subject: Creator Program submission - agent demo ([handle])

Hi Cognee team,

I'm [name], an AI builder and creator in the UK, posting as [handle] on [platform]. I build working agents and automations - recent builds include [build 1] and [build 2] - and post short demo videos of them.

My demo of [build] is live here: [link]. It's at [impressions] impressions after [days] days; I'll send the final 7-day organic numbers once it crosses the window.

Happy to verify my UK region and add the paid-partnership disclosure. What else do you need to approve it?

[name]

> **Know before you send:** Cash tiers run $160 (1k-5k impressions) up to $8,000 (100k+), paid within 5 business days of approval. Below the 1k tier an approved post earns $40 in cloud credit, not cash. The demo must be your own real agent, memory or code.


### Pitch template 2 - AI tool brands (Lovable, ElevenLabs, Creatify)

Use when you already have a video of their product working (days 4, 10, 12, 17, 24, 27). Lead with the build, not the ask.


**DM version** (To a partnerships or creator lead)

Hey [name] - I run [handle], an AI-build account ([90-day impressions] impressions in the last 90 days, [avg views] average views per video). I already use [product] in real builds - last week I [specific thing built with it]. I have a video idea that would show it off well: [one-line concept]. Do you run affiliate or sponsored creator deals?


**Email version** (To partnerships@ or the affiliate contact)

Subject: Creator partnership - [product] in a real AI build

Hi [name / team],

I'm [name], an AI-niche creator in the UK ([handle]). My audience is builders and students who want working demos, not hype - [90-day impressions] impressions in the last 90 days, [avg views] average views per video, [engagement rate] engagement.

I already use [product]: I built [specific build] with it here - [video link]. I'd like to make [one-line concept] next.

If you have an affiliate or ambassador programme, send me the terms. If you do sponsored creator posts, my rate is [rate] per video. Either way I only post it if the build genuinely works - that's the whole account.

[name] - [handle] - [portfolio link]

> **What each programme pays:** Lovable: up to $100 per referral. ElevenLabs: 22% of a referred subscription's first 12 months via PartnerStack (about a 90-day validation delay). Creatify: 25% recurring commission, 30% above $5k/month in sales. Commission-only - you earn when viewers buy, so these fit tutorial and comparison content, not one-off hype.

### Pitch template 4 - Marketplaces (JoinBrands, Billo)

These are profile-first platforms: the bio below is your shopfront, the application message is what you paste under each brief. Both are open to UK creators 18+.


**Profile bio** (Paste into your creator profile)

UK-based UGC creator in the AI and tools niche. I demo AI products to an audience of builders and students - [90-day impressions] impressions in the last 90 days, [avg views] average views per video. Vertical video: talking-head or screen-recorded demos, [X]-day turnaround. Best fit: tech, AI, SaaS and student brands. Portfolio: [link]


**Application message** (Under each brief you apply to)

Hi [brand] - [handle] here, UK AI-niche creator. Your brief fits my audience exactly: [one line on why]. I'd shoot [one-line concept] - [talking head / demo / voiceover], delivered within [X] days. Recent work: [link 1], [link 2]. My rate is [rate] per video. Happy to start this week.

> **Set your floor:** JoinBrands advertises $60+ per video but takes a 20% platform fee, so treat the headline as gross. Billo pays per video with rates varying by brief. Set your [rate] no lower than what an evening of your time is worth - these briefs are one-off cash, not relationships.
