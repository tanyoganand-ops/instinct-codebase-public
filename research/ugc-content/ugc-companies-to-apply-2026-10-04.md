## 1. Growpins
- Links: https://growpins.ai/join-partner-program (form), https://growpins.ai/creator-partnership (rates, rights)
- What: AI marketing/SEO tool. Creator track pays for videos, articles, podcasts or newsletter features.
- Pay: $200 base per approved piece, bonus once a piece passes 10,000 views. You keep ownership.
- Wants: one 5-minute application covering both partner tracks (affiliate and creator). Judged on quality, not audience size. Content review about 3 days.
- Route: online form, reply in 2-3 business days. Rolling.
- Phone-doable: yes.
- Caveats: the page mentions two approval requirements I could not read in full; check them on the form. Pick the creator track if you want fixed pay.

## 2. GenieAI (Genie Content Creators)
- Link: https://www.genieai.co/genie-content-creators
- What: legal AI company ("the risk management system for contract velocity"). Pays creators to explain it. This is NOT general AI content.
- Pay: $260 per published/accepted video, no minimum, no cap. Referral rewards: $130 Pro plan, $650 Business plan, $1,300 Enterprise. Free GenieAI Pro account for active creators. Paid monthly by bank transfer in local currency.
- Route (their 4 steps):
  1. Apply: send your channel or a sample of work. Screened on craft and clarity, not follower count. Decision within 5 working days.
  2. Get the kit: free Pro account, monthly briefs, brand assets, B-roll, referral link, creator dashboard.
  3. Make the video: pick a brief or pitch your own. Get the concept approved BEFORE recording.
  4. Submit: drop the link in your dashboard. Reviewed within 5 working days, paid in the next monthly run.
- Channels: YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, X or your own platform. Long or short form both count, so your LinkedIn is a valid channel.
- Formats: platform tutorials, honest reviews, how-tos, 60s tips, AI/business commentary, AI and society explainers.
- Deadline: rolling.
- Phone-doable: yes (screen recording + talking).
- Caveats: the apply form URL, T&Cs ("T&Cs apply"), usage rights and age limit are not on the page. No UK restriction stated. Your sample should be a legal-doc explainer with a "not legal advice" line.

## 3. Knowunity creator programme
- Links: https://knowunity.co.uk/creators/apply (UK form), https://knowunity.co.uk/creators (info). Same form on https://knowunity.com/creators/apply
- What: study app, UK launch confirmed (App Store GB listing). Short TikTok videos about it.
- Pay: fixed fee per approved video regardless of performance. Amount not stated.
- Form asks: confirm requirements; name and contact; your best social account (TikTok/IG/Snapchat/Shorts, follower count doesn't matter); upload a 30-90s casual talking video (MP4/MOV/WebM, up to 100 MB; any topic, existing video allowed); optional "referred by".
- Requirements: resident in a country where Knowunity is available (UK is), age 16-22, post on TikTok 3-5 times a week, no competing study-app deals. Under 18 needs parent or guardian consent.
- Deadline: rolling. Phone-doable: yes.
- Caveats: 3-5 posts a week next to uni. Ask the per-video fee before committing.

## 4. The UGC Agency
- Link: https://theugc.agency/creators/
- What: agency matching creators to brand briefs. Says 4,900+ vetted creators, 200+ brands, 40+ countries.
- Their 4 steps: 1 apply (1 minute: niche, socials, rate); 2 profile goes into their matching tool within an hour; 3 matched when a brief fits your profile and rate, and they tell you what the campaign pays; 4 paid by direct bank transfer after the brand approves.
- Wants: one or two sample videos help, brand-new creators can send casual clips.
- Deadline: rolling; some creators wait over a week for a match.
- Phone-doable: yes.
- Caveats: no guaranteed work. Other form fields beyond niche/socials/rate are not visible. Put 100-150 GBP for AI niche as your rate.

## 5. ThetaWave Creator Program
- Link: https://thetawave.ai/creator-program (same page at https://thepro.app/creator-program)
- What: student AI study tool. Short TikTok/Reels about how you study with it.
- Route: apply in 5 minutes (who you are, link to socials, one video idea). They reply within 7 days. If it fits: short async interview, then a 30-minute onboarding call with a coach. First payout typically within the first 2 weeks.
- Pay: base payout per approved post + view bonuses at 100K/500K/1M + monthly leaderboard. "Up to $5,500/month" is for top creators, not typical. Base rate not published, confirmed at onboarding.
- Wants: students, comfortable on camera, 3-5 videos a week, 5-10 hours a week. Bonus, not required: a 50K+ view video, study audience, editing skills.
- Perks: coaching, creator certificate, LinkedIn-ready entry, recommendation letter on request.
- "Student creators worldwide": no UK blocker. Deadline: rolling. Phone-doable: yes.
- Caveats: heaviest workload on the list. Get the per-post base rate in writing before accepting.
