# First five UGC applications

Drafts for you to edit and submit yourself. No accounts created, forms filled or applications submitted.

Prepared 4 October 2026 from the UGC master, ease comparison and a fresh read of the public application pages. Exact fields below are live field labels where accessible. The master does not contain full form questionnaires. Unavailable later steps are clearly separated from verified fields.

## Use your numbers honestly

- Around 1,000 followers. Put the actual count for the platform a form asks about. Do not treat LinkedIn followers as Instagram/TikTok followers or YouTube subscribers.
- Around 500k total impressions/views as you reported, with roughly 180k LinkedIn views and 270k YouTube views. These are approximate historical figures, not monthly reach, current average views or guaranteed results.
- UK-based. Your age, city, handles, availability, rates and portfolio links stay in [brackets].
- Your older YouTube Shorts work is evidence of short-form experience, not evidence of an active AI audience. Label it accurately.
- Replace every bracket before submitting. Only link samples you actually have. New unpaid examples should say "spec sample - not sponsored". Do not pass concept images or invented app screens off as completed client work.

Reusable short bio, for a real free-text field:

> I'm a UK-based creator and builder focused on AI tools. I've got around 1,000 followers and roughly 500k total impressions/views across my content, including about 180k LinkedIn views and 270k YouTube views. I want to make practical demos: one clear problem, the actual tool on screen and an honest result.

## Reusable 30-60 second video pitch

About 105 words before bracket substitutions, roughly 45-55 seconds at a natural pace. Record simply: eye-level camera, clear audio, then a short real screen demo. Do not add a demo you have not made.

> Hi, I'm [Name], a UK-based creator and builder focused on AI tools. I've got around a thousand followers and roughly half a million total impressions and views across my content, including about 180 thousand LinkedIn views and 270 thousand YouTube views.
>
> My angle is simple: show the tool doing one useful job. No feature-list waffle, no made-up results.
>
> For [programme], I'd start with [specific concept]. I'd show the problem, the real workflow and one thing to watch out for.
>
> Here's a quick example of my style: [show a real sample].
>
> I'd like to make clear, honest content from your briefs.

### Swap the concept line

- **The UGC Agency:** "a short AI-app demo with a clear before-and-after task." Use only if a later brief or contact asks for a pitch; the current form does not require one.
- **TapLab:** "a phone-shot tech demo for the brand's own channels, with a clear hook and the product on screen."
- **Ask Maeve:** "a study demo using a topic I actually know, checking the answer against the original material."
- **Growpins:** "one seller task from start to finish using a workflow confirmed in your brief."
- **GenieAI:** "a sample-NDA walkthrough, agreed before filming and clearly labelled as a product demo, not legal advice."

If there is no real example to show yet, replace that sentence with: "My proposed format is a short hook, a screen demo and an honest takeaway." Do not imply an unfinished sample is ready.

## Final check before each submission

- All brackets replaced; age and relevant-platform follower counts truthful.
- Links work and show your own work. Historical Shorts and spec samples labelled honestly.
- No claims about student status, clients, conversion results, regular views or delivery speed added without evidence.
- You choose the rate, marketing consent and contractual consent yourself.
- A programme's 18+ or guardian conditions are real requirements, not something to work around in a form.
