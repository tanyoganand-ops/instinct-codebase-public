# Free routes and compute: V05-V07

## 2026-10-01 - Which free routes deserve testing?

**Question:** What legitimate free routes refresh, preserve anime style and offer enough compute for useful animation?

**Findings:** The 17:06 BST comparison ranked hosted HF Wan first, keyframe-plus-motion second, and Kaggle notebooks third. Wan was then tested successfully later that day; the other proposed workflow details were not all tested. A style-bearing keyframe was recommended over pure text-to-video. The successful Wan sample used a newly generated keyframe; it did not validate every suggested still-image service.

Other reported candidates:

| Route | Historical research finding | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Colab | Free GPU hours were not guaranteed; Google-account access was blocked in the earlier video attempt | No video notebook benchmark; claims about web-UI restrictions were not independently verified in the comparison |
| Modal | $30/month free compute credit, cited from pricing | Wan deployment not built; estimated 50-150 clips was speculative and should not be treated as capacity |
| Lightning.ai | 80 free GPU hours on signup, described as a one-time allowance | Not a refreshing daily route; signup and render not tested |
| Tensor.art | Daily credits reported to top up to 50; free Wan workflow described as 3-second clips | Sign-in challenge remained unresolved in follow-up; no output test completed |
| SeaArt | Approximately 130 daily stamina | Later live popup confirmed the amount, but video cost and watermark stayed unknown |

**Sources:** Historical comparison at 17:06 BST and follow-ups at 17:13 and 21:03 BST, 1 October. URLs supplied in that comparison:

**Verdict/next:** Prefer measured hosted output now; treat Kaggle/Modal as separate engineering experiments. One-time trials do not meet the refreshing-allowance goal. Do not treat a proposed deployment or quota conversion as completed video capacity.

## 2026-10-01 - Which routes should stay low priority or unknown?

**Question:** Which apparent freebies are weak leads rather than usable daily capacity?

**Findings:** The comparison reported that Kling had no regular free-plan credits and that login credits were event-dependent. Hailuo and Vidu allowance/watermark claims were third-party-only. Civitai was reported unavailable from the UK in this workflow. Pika, Genmo, Haiper, Domo, Kaiber, Morph, Grok Imagine and Qwen web demo were explicitly left unverified.

The original comparison also called Bing/Sora retired and fal/Replicate free credits unavailable, but the recovered summary does not preserve sufficient precise primary-source evidence to adopt those broad product-status statements here as verified facts. They remain leads to check if they matter again, not permanent exclusions.

**Sources:** Historical comparison, 1 October 2026, 17:06 BST:
- https://kling.ai/docs/point-policy

No exact third-party article URLs were preserved for the Hailuo/Vidu claims. They are therefore not promoted into reliable policy records.

**Verdict/next:** Do not budget output from unverified freebies. Recheck a named service's current official terms only when it becomes a real candidate. No duplicate-account farming or identity/region evasion is part of this plan.

## 2026-10-02 - Does the tested stack meet the two-minute daily target?

**Question:** Do the actual results justify the earlier daily-volume estimates?

**Findings:** Wan produced about 5 seconds; LTX produced about 5 seconds and added T2V capability. The anonymous quota probe stopped further generation after roughly two clips across the recorded session. SeaArt produced no clip. Kaggle had not passed phone verification or run a notebook. Dreamina remained blank. PixVerse had accepted a job but had no finished result at its closeout.

**Sources:** Historical result reports on 1-2 October, detailed in the linked topic files:
- https://huggingface.co/spaces/Lightricks/ltx-video-distilled
- https://huggingface.co/spaces/zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster
- https://dreamina.capcut.com/ai-tool/home

**Verdict/next:** No evidence establishes two minutes of worthwhile free video per day. Earlier 4-8 or 5-10 clip estimates should not be repeated as measured output. The next useful research is a real quota/quality benchmark after legitimate access is complete, followed by a licensing check. None of these research entries authorizes signup or spending.
