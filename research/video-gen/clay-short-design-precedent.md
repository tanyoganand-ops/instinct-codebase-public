# Clay Short Series - Design Precedent

## 1. Direction
Build a 15-second, silent vertical comparison in pastel clay on a pale, clean background. Keep the frame light and legible; use tasteful 3D depth, deliberate motion, and one clear hero idea at a time. The look should feel dimensional, not like a camera ride or a glass-heavy interface.

## 2. Hard rules

### Must
- Deliver at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, silent.
- Keep all essential content inside the safe rectangle: x=48–888, y=288–1248.
- Use Anton for display type; never use thin fonts.
- Meet these minimum type sizes: source rail 48 px; benchmark names 56 px; verdict 72 px; CTA 64 px.
- Keep the background pale, clean, and quiet. Use glass sparingly, only for edges or accents.
- Use no more than 1–2 purposeful hero objects in a scene.
- Use the v8 raster lockups for Claude and Gemini logos.

### Never
- No hard cuts, fly-ins, spins, or zooms.
- No static frames: keep motion purposeful throughout, without adding motion for its own sake.
- No camera-journey treatment, orbiting 3D props, or porcelain finish.
- No heavy liquid glass or busy, dark backgrounds.
- No corporate-A SVG logo swap.

## 3. Taste calibration

### Liked
- **v9 3D look:** layered depth, a perspective turn at the benchmark handoff, directional shadows, and stronger refraction. P said they "really really like the 3d looks"; retain the dimensionality and purposeful handoff, not a continuous camera journey.
- **B3 motion discipline:** the principle of never leaving a static frame is useful. B3's visuals were rejected, so carry forward the motion discipline only, not its visual design.
- **Content-layer parallax:** keep this as a technique for future videos. Apply it to content layers, not drifting backgrounds.

### Rejected
- **B5 camera journey:** "Nah." Do not move the viewer through the scene.
- **B4 orbiting 3D props:** "not tasteful enough." Avoid decorative orbiting objects.
- **B2 porcelain look:** "Meh." Keep the clay pastel and tactile, not polished porcelain.
- **Heavy liquid glass and busy/dark backgrounds:** "use in moderation, bg is bad." Keep glass to accents and the background pale, clean, and restrained.

## 4. Toolkit
Use these selectively to support the story:
- Content-layer parallax; never background drift.
- DOF racks to direct attention.
- Cast and contact shadows for grounded depth.
- Matte relighting to shape clay forms while preserving a soft, tactile finish.
- Refraction for glass only, and in moderation.
- Real 3D geometry sparingly, for 1–2 purposeful hero objects at most.
- Motion discipline: soft bounce drops, count-up scores, coordinated exits and enters, and morph-into-next-scene handoffs. Keep each movement clear and controlled.
